1. What this app is

Alltag entschlüsselt Uploader ("the app") is a private tool run by Berkan Gül ("the operator") for the operator's own YouTube channel "Alltag entschlüsselt". It creates German motion-graphics explainer videos about how everyday things work, uploads them and Shorts cut from them to that channel through the YouTube Data API, and reads the channel's own statistics and comments to improve future videos.

2. Who may use it

The app is not offered to the public. Only the operator uses it, with the operator's own Google account and YouTube channel. There is no sign-up and no service for other people.

3. Content

All videos are created by the app itself: code-rendered motion graphics, an AI text-to-speech voice and scripts written with AI assistance; every factual claim is checked against sources before publishing. Videos are simplified explanations for general education and are not medical, legal, financial or professional advice. The operator is responsible for every published video and follows the YouTube Community Guidelines.
