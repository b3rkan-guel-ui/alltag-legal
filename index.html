Alltag entschlüsselt Uploader

A private tool used only by the operator of the YouTube channel Alltag entschlüsselt (German motion-graphics explainer videos about how everyday things work). It uploads the channel's own videos and Shorts and reads the channel's own statistics and public comments to learn which videos work best.
